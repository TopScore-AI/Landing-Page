Our vision

Every student deserves a tutor who asks the right question, not just gives the right answer.

TopScore AI exists because quality, one-on-one tutoring has always been a privilege reserved for those who can afford it. We're building the closest thing to it — in a phone, for the cost of a bus fare.

Why we built TopScore AI

A private tutor is still the most effective way to learn — someone who notices exactly where your understanding breaks down and adjusts in real time. But a private tutor is also expensive, and only available for the hours they can spare. Most students in Kenya never get that kind of attention, no matter how much they need it.

We built TopScore AI to close that gap. Not with a chatbot that dumps answers, but with a tutor that behaves the way a good human tutor would: asking the next question, drawing the diagram, checking your reasoning against the actual curriculum you're sitting — CBC, 8-4-4/KCSE, or Cambridge IGCSE.

What we believe

  • Understanding beats answers. A fact you were guided to is worth more than one you were handed. Our tutor teaches through questions first.
  • Accuracy is non-negotiable. Every explanation is checked against real curriculum standards and real diagrams — never improvised.
  • Learning is visual. Some ideas only click when you can see them. We treat diagrams as core to teaching, not decoration.
  • Every student deserves this, not just those who can pay for a private tutor. That's why a free plan will always exist.

Built in Kenya, for Kenyan students

We're based in Nairobi, close to the classrooms, the curricula, and the students we build for. That closeness shapes every decision — from which past papers we prioritise to how the app behaves on a patchy connection.

Where we're going

We're still early. Every term, more subjects, more past papers, and more curriculum-vetted diagrams are added to the library, and the tutor gets better at knowing exactly where a student is stuck. If you want to be part of that, the fastest way in is to open the app.
